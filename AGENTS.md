# abase

A shared knowledge base and coordination system for Amir's coding agents, across his companies and projects. `BACKGROUND.md` has the goal and his starting thoughts.

## Discovery stage

Nothing is built yet. Amir is working out what this should be, and he drops notes into chat as they come to him: short, unordered, often half-formed.

- Capture every note he gives you without being asked. Add it to `NOTES.md` under today's date, in his words, tidied only enough to read cleanly. Record what he said, not your expansion of it; if you have a thought or question about a note, say it in chat.
- Then commit and push: `git add -A && git commit -m "Capture notes: <topic>" && git push`.
- Reply in one line saying what you captured.
- Don't build anything (code, schemas, scaffolding) until Amir says discovery is over.

This repo is public on GitHub. Write down where sensitive things live, never their contents: no credentials, customer data, or company numbers such as revenue or metrics.
